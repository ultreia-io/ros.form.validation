# Contributing

Open an issue describing the proposed change, with the affected gear and form version.

Include a small anonymised example or a link to the official form. Do not share private submitted data.

Use `feature/` branches based on `develop`. Submit pull requests to `develop` and assign Tony Chemit (`ultreiaio`).

Edit `README.md` directly. Regenerate and commit `man/*.Rd` and `NAMESPACE` with their roxygen sources.

Follow the [maintainer guide](https://ultreia-io.github.io/ros.form.validation/DEVELOPMENT.html) for local checks and releases.

By contributing, you agree to license your contributions under GPL-3.
